Because she rediscovered the long-forgotten model at one of our bag swap events. Since then, the two have only existed as an inseparable duo, and human Laura was constantly being asked whether FREITAG could please bring back bag Laura. So we did. And because it's such a good story, we've made Flagship Laura the face of our campaign. Together with nine other Lauras and one Laurin. No joke.
