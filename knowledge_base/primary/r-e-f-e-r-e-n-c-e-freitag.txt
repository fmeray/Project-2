Play Video 00:00:00 Mute Fullscreen Play Video 00:00:00 Mute Fullscreen THE CRAFT IS THE MESSAGE R E F E R E N C E is about discovering, rethinking and mastering a craft. At FREITAG, that craft is transforming used truck tarps. For R E F E R E N C E, the raw material is recontextualized almost beyond recognition. The message: truck tarps can hold their own against traditional luxury materials — especially when worked with dedication, an obsessive attention to detail and years of experience. Redesigned from scratch, the line combines three decades of upcycling expertise with craftsmanship at its finest. BAGS WITH ATTITUDE Anyone who chooses a R E F E R E N C E one-off bag is making a statement: for resource-conscious material choices and timeless elegance, and against throwaway culture. A clear demonstration that style and sustainability can go hand in hand. The used tarps for these one-offs are sourced throughout Europe and rigorously selected. In an elaborate, hands-on process, the one-offs are produced in small batches by European production partners, with some custom-made components even manufactured in Switzerland. A FACTORY FIND The central visual feature of R E F E R E N C E is its striking magnetic clasp with punched branding. The inspiration came from the place where FREITAG's history and identity can be felt at first hand: the bag factory in Zurich-Oerlikon. Amid tarp offcuts, box cutters and oversized washing machines, product designer Anne Bachmann came across an unusual toothed piece of metal: an adjustment hook for heavy-duty shelving. An industrial artifact, recontextualized and refined. Typically FREITAG. THE BAG FIRST-AID KIT Because wear and fatigue are part of a life well lived, every R E F E R E N C E one-off comes with a repair kit: a practical pouch designed to be taken apart, made from the same individual tarp as its bag and containing a piece of lining and sewing thread. Take those out, and the repair kit can also be used for cards, keys and the like. VERSATILE BAGS, SMART DETAILS R E F E R E N C E bags don't just retell the FREITAG story: their trapezoid silhouettes and outward-folded side seams also introduce a new design language. The handles contain no glue and feel soft in the hand, while the carefully thought-out recycled polyester interior, with practical pockets and thoughtful details, completes this new generation of bags. FIVE COLORS, ENDLESS PATINA The constant rigors of Europe’s transit routes leave scratches, scuffs, wrinkles and lines on the truck tarps — all traces of a life in motion. This patina tells a story. And makes every bag one of a kind. R E F E R E N C E launches in a palette rich in patina, with whites, silvers, reds and blues — the finest shades the transit routes have to offer — alongside a limited edition in black. Interview im Detail What inspired you when developing R E F E R E N C E? People always come first — their needs and their everyday lives. I also try to find contrasts and bring them together; with R E F E R E N C E, for example, it was glamour and the street. The FREITAG factory is also an endless source of inspiration for me. I keep discovering raw beauty where you least expect it. What is R E F E R E N C E to you? What is R E F E R E N C E to you? An answer to the question of how our beautiful, weathered, favorite material can be interpreted for today. R E F E R E N C E puts truck tarp center stage as a material in its own right. And brings its raw materiality together with craftsmanship at the highest level. What makes tarp such a special material? What makes tarp such a special material? That every one is different. Each piece has a character of its own — you never know what you're going to get. The material forces you to give up control. With R E F E R E N C E, I wanted to find out where the limits of working with used tarps lie. How would you describe the aesthetics of R E F E R E N C E? How would you describe the aesthetics of R E F E R E N C E? R E F E R E N C E is elevated, strong and raw. Elegant, but unconventional too. Detail-obsessed. And very much of the moment. But it’s about more than the visual appearance; it engages all the senses: sounds like the snap of a closing flap, the smell, the feel as you run your hand over it — all of that shapes the aesthetics of a FREITAG product.
