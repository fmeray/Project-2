Today, everything we do is about leveraging smart design, resource-efficient production and distribution processes, a tailor-made range of services, and targeted material innovations to help us gradually move away from the linear economy. The 2025 FREITAG Impact Statements offer a snapshot of where FREITAG currently stands on this journey. The 33 statements show the key actions, facts, figures, progress, and challenges from 2025. The report follows the typical life cycle of a FREITAG bag: from the design process and choice of materials to production and distribution, the use phase and the question of what happens at the end of the product’s longest-possible life. The FREITAG Impact Statements are intended to inform, inspire reflection, and spark ideas. Thanks for reading. PURPOSE AND STRATEGY FREITAG is guided by the principles of the cyclical economy. «We think and act in cycles» is our Purpose, and this guiding idea shapes our products, services and business decisions. The circular economy aims to replace the linear production processes that are still common today. In a linear economic system, raw materials are extracted and used to make products that are manufactured, sold, consumed and thrown away. In the circular economy, by contrast, products and materials are kept in circulation, which means significantly fewer primary raw materials are used and less waste is generated than in a linear economic system. We understand the circular economy as a holistic approach that encompasses the entire material and product cycle: from smart design and the use of waste materials, fair and resource-efficient production, responsible distribution and and an extended use phase — and where possible, multiple rounds of recycling. As an integral part of our circularity strategy, we are working to achieve a balance across the entire supply chain between environmental and social responsibility and economic success. To do this, we aim to further reduce our footprint, ensure that all the materials we use are safe, and make the supply chain fairer. WE THINK AND ACT IN CYCLES. FROM TRUCK TO BAG – THE FREITAG VALUE CHAIN The journey from truck to FREITAG one-off bag essentially consists of five steps: First, we search all over Europe for beautiful used truck tarps. The tarps are stripped of straps and buckles and tested to make sure they are safe to use. In huge washing machines, each tarp is cleaned of the grime it picked up on the road. When the bag parts are cut, the look of each individual one-off takes shape. The bag parts are sewn into finished bags in Europe, checked and photographed in Zurich, and then shipped all over the world. The life of a FREITAG one-off starts much earlier, though, and will hopefully end many years after purchase. It can be divided into six chapters and six guiding questions: Design : How do you design everyday companions that are as durable and timeless as possible while keeping their impact on the planet as low as possible throughout their entire life cycle? Material : How can we integrate as many used, discarded or recycled materials as possible in our high-quality products? Production : How can we design our production processes to be as efficient, resource-saving and socially responsible as possible? Distribution : How do we get our one-offs to end consumers in a way that is customer-focused, efficient and resource-saving? Use : What services can extend the use phase of our products? End of life / Closing the loop : What happens when a product reaches the end of its life — and how can material loops be closed? These questions shape our day-to-day work at FREITAG. The chapters of the 2026 Impact Statements follow this order. 1. DESIGN At FREITAG, we see product design as the most powerful lever for our circular ambitions. Most of a product’s environmental impact is established as early as the development phase — which is why we design not only the product, but its entire life cycle. Our approach is holistic: we combine the individual and timeless character of our one-offs with a production designed for durability, repairability, and material loops . Through smart concepts, we aim to prevent the products themselves from becoming waste for as long as possible. WASTE IS A DESIGN ERROR. 2. MATERIAL It all started with used truck tarps, discarded car seat belts and old bicycle inner tubes. FREITAG has steadily expanded and further developed the sourcing and reusing of materials that, in one way or another, arise as waste. While upcycled tarps still play the lead role, our product range also includes, for example, iPhone cases made from worn-out ski boots and bags made from airbags that were once installed in cars. When choosing and sourcing materials, we look for high quality, durability and robustness — and we make sure that, despite their previous life on the road, they do not contain any substances that might be of concern. WASTE IS OUR RESOURCE. 3. PRODUCTION Our production can be divided into three stages. First, the dirty waste material from the road is checked, sorted, dismantled and thoroughly washed. The second stage includes both cutting the tarps and all manufacturing and sewing work, which is carried out by our long-standing European production partners. Finally, the finished one-offs undergo another thorough quality check, they are photographed, given an identification number and registered in our systems. At FREITAG, our production processes focus on quality, resource efficiency and social responsibility. PRODUCTION WITH PRINCIPLES. 4. DISTRIBUTION & SALES There are three ways that FREITAG one-offs make their way from Zurich out into the big wide world and into the hands of urban individualists: through 30 FREITAG stores in 11 countries, the FREITAG online store based in Zurich-Oerlikon, and around 300 retail partners in 25 countries. In distribution, we focus on an optimal customer experience, efficient processes, and avoiding excessive inventory. FREITAG doesn't do seasonal collections or ever-faster trend cycles. Instead, sales follow the «Never Out Of Stock» principle: most of our products are manufactured and offered over many years. COMMERCE WITH A CONSCIENCE 5. USE Our commitment to the circular economy does not end when a product is finished or sold. FREITAG one-offs are robust and durable. And that is why we want them to be used as intensively and for as long as possible. We do this by repairing products, renting them out, or making sure they find new owners when they are no longer needed. FREITAG offers a range of tailor-made services for exactly that purpose. At the center is our globally established repair service and the provision of free spare parts , supplemented by various exchange and resale offers as well as FREITAG RENT . At the same time, we are further expanding our take-back service so that our first circular products can genuinely remain in closed material loops. WE MAKE PRODUCTS THAT LAST. 6. CLOSING THE LOOP Products can only return to the material cycle at the end of their lives if they are designed for circularity, made from circular materials, and supported by well-thought-out services. FREITAG has already closed several material loops—with its compostable F-ABRIC clothing line, for example, or the CIRC-CASES and Mono[PA6] bags , which can be recycled as a whole at the end of their lives. With Circular Tarp , our long-term material innovation project, we are laying the groundwork for a future in which FREITAG one-offs made from used truck tarps can also be recycled. WASTE IS A DESIGN ERROR. (2) THE F-CREW The people who work for FREITAG — the F-Crew — are the engine behind our Purpose. We are committed to a diverse and inclusive work environment where different perspectives come together, guided by shared values that shape our everyday actions. So that we can continue to develop as an organization, the FREITAG Circularity & Sustainability team works with specialists from across the organization to systematically build knowledge and develop measures around the circular economy. In this way, the transition to a circular economy becomes a shared learning project: only as a strong, empowered community can we truly realize our vision of a circular future. THE F-CREW IS OUR ENGINE.
