# Market trends — circular bags and textiles

## EU textile consumption and waste
The European Environment Agency estimates that apparent consumption of clothing, footwear and household textiles averaged 19 kg per person in the EU in 2022, up from 17 kg in 2019. EU countries generated approximately 6.94 million tonnes of textile waste in 2022 (about 16 kg per person). These figures describe the broader textile sector, not FREITAG's footprint.
Source: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/analysis/publications/circularity-of-the-eu-textiles-value-chain-in-numbers

## Reuse, repair and circularity
The EEA argues that longer product lifetimes, reuse, repair and recycling can help reduce the environmental pressures of textile production and consumption. Upcycling is one circular approach, but the mere use of reclaimed material does not prove that an individual product is carbon-neutral or environmentally superior.
Source: https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/topics/in-depth/textiles

## EU circular textile policy
The European Commission's sustainable and circular textiles strategy includes initiatives on product durability, repairability, recycling, producer responsibility and environmental information. The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation provides a framework for product-group-specific measures.
Source: https://environment.ec.europa.eu/strategy/textiles-strategy_en

## Digital Product Passports
The European Commission identifies textiles among priority product groups in its ESPR Working Plan 2025–2030. Textile-specific Digital Product Passport requirements depend on future delegated rules; their inclusion in the work plan does not mean that every textile product is already required to carry a passport. The Commission gives an indicative Q4 2027 timeline for adoption of the relevant textile delegated act, subject to change.
Sources:
https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/textile-apparel_en
https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/digital-product-passport/explore-our-faqs_en

## Implications for FREITAG content
- Circular design, material reuse, and product longevity are relevant industry themes.
- Avoid treating general textile statistics as measurements of FREITAG products.
- Do not make unsupported claims of carbon savings, biodegradability, or legal compliance.
- Verify new regulations and dates before publication.
