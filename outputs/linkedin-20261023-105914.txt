🌍 At FREITAG, our focus on circular materials is essential to our mission. Since 1993, we’ve turned discarded truck tarps into distinctive bags, creating durable and practical companions for everyday life.

Our commitment to upcycling goes further; we are dedicated to closing material loops. Innovations like the compostable F-ABRIC clothing line and the 100% recyclable Mono[PA6] Backpack are key steps on our path to a circular economy. By using mono-materials and embracing thoughtful designs, we aim to prolong the life of our products and minimize waste.

At FREITAG, thinking and acting in cycles is more than just a goal—it’s our way of working. We invite you to join us in this pursuit of sustainability, where every material has the potential for a new life. Together, we can create a more circular future.
