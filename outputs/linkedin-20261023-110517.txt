🌍 At FREITAG, we believe that waste is simply a resource waiting to be transformed. Since 1993, we've been committed to a circular economy by designing bags and accessories from used materials, primarily discarded truck tarps, but increasingly from circular materials.

Our innovative approach doesn't just extend the life of these robust materials; it also reduces environmental impact. For instance, our Mono[PA6] Backpack, made entirely from a single recyclable material, marks a significant step towards true circularity. We're continuously researching new materials and processes, like our compostable F‑ABRIC clothing line and the CIRC-CASE phone sleeve, ensuring that we live up to our mantra: "Waste is a design error."

As we gear up for the next 30 years, we aim to close even more material loops—creating products designed for longevity and recyclability. Join us on this journey to redefine what it means to be sustainable by thinking and acting in cycles. Together, we can shape a future where nothing goes to waste.
