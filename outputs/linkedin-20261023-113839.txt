🌍 **Embracing the Circular Future at FREITAG** 🎒

At FREITAG, our journey began in 1993 with two brothers crafting bags from discarded truck tarps. Today, we’re proudly pioneering the circular economy by using not just upcycled materials, but increasingly circular ones. 

Our latest innovations, like the **Mono[PA6] Backpack**—a fully recyclable product made entirely from PA6 nylon—signal a new chapter in our commitment to sustainability. Each step in our design process focuses on longevity and material circularity, ensuring that our products can be repurposed instead of discarded.

We're not just creating bags; we’re extending lives. With initiatives like our repair service and innovative recycling projects, we aim to close the loop on materials, ensuring they serve many purposes throughout their lifecycle.

Join us as we continue to explore and develop smarter materials and practices that bolster a sustainable future. Together, let’s think and act in cycles. 💚
